## 3. Defence Forces Static GK and Organizational Facts

Core static GK related to the structure, notable figures, and history of India’s defence forces is emphasized.

- **Indian Air Force (IAF)** origin in 1932 as “Royal Indian Air Force”; first aircraft: **Vapti**.
- Independence-era milestones:
  - Air Force renamed Indian Air Force (26 Jan 1950),
  - Crest inscribed in **Devanagari script** since 1979 with “भारतीय वायु सेना.”
- **Flying Officer Nirmaljit Singh Sekhon**: Only IAF officer awarded the **Param Vir Chakra** (PVC).
- Highest operational fighter airbase: **Nyoma Air Force Station (2025)**.
- Highest airstrip: **Dault Beg Oldi**, used for transport and helicopters but **not fighter aircraft**.
- Largest Air Force base: **Hindon Air Force Station**.
- **IAF Aerobatic Teams**:
  - **Surya Kiran** (Hawk MK132 aircraft, 9 aircraft total),
  - **Sarang** helicopter team uses HAL Dhruv helicopters.

## 7. Missile Systems: Types, Launch Modes, and Range

The video offers a thorough classification of missile types by launch mode, trajectory, speed, and country of origin.

### Missile Classification by Launch and Target

| Type | Description | Example(s) |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Surface-to-Surface | Launched from land/sea, targets ground | **Agni series, Prithvi, BrahMos** |
| Surface-to-Air | Launched from ground, targets airborne targets | **Akash, MR-SAM** |
| Air-to-Surface | Launched from aircraft, targets ground | **Helina missile, Jaguar aircraft** |
| Air-to-Air | Launched from aircraft, targets aircraft | **Astra missile** |
| Ship-to-Ship | Launched from warships/submarines, targets ships | **BrahMos** |
| Ship-to-Air | Surface-to-air defence missile from ship | **Barak** |
| Submarine-launched | Launched from submarines | **K-15, Sagarika** |
| Shoulder-fired | Man-portable anti-air systems | **Igla-S, FIM-92 Stinger** |

### Missile Classification by Trajectory

| Missile Type | Trajectory Characteristic | Example(s) |
|--------------|---------------------------|------------|
| Ballistic | Exits atmosphere and follows gravity-controlled parabolic path | **Agni, Prithvi, Shaurya, K-15 Sagarika, Pralay** |
| Cruise | Flies within atmosphere at low altitude with guidance | **BrahMos, Nirbhay** |

### Missile Classification by Speed

| Classification | Speed Range | Example(s) |
|---------------|-------------|------------|
| Subsonic | Speed < Mach 1 | **Prithvi** |
| Supersonic | Mach 1–5 | **BrahMos** |
| Hypersonic | Above Mach 5 | **Shaurya, Sagarika** |

### Selected Key Missile Facts

- **BrahMos**: Supersonic cruise missile (~Mach 3), Indo-Russian joint development, range ~450 km.
- **Agni-3**: Intermediate-range ballistic missile with nuclear capability.
- **TARA**: India's first indigenous glide weapon system, tested with Jaguar aircraft.
- **Helina**: Fire-and-forget anti-tank missile integrated with SU-30 and LCH Prachand.
- **ASRAAM**: Advanced Short-Range Air-to-Air Missile integrated with MiG-29, speed ~Mach 3, developed by MBDA.

## 8. Recent Defence Operations and Exercises

- **Operation Sankalp (2019)**: Indian Navy safeguarded merchant vessels in Gulf of Oman/Gulf of Aden.
- **Operation Urja**: Deployment of 5+ frontline warships to secure 22 Indian-bound energy vessels.
- **Operation Triple 7**: Multilateral partnership involving 77 countries across 7 continents.
- **Operation Amistad**: Humanitarian support to Venezuela after twin earthquakes using C-17 Globemaster and Bhishma Cubes.
