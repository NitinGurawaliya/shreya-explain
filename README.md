### **4. Static GK: Indian Air Force (IAF) History & Facts**
* **Establishment:**
  * Originally **Royal Indian Air Force** (1932).
  * First aircraft: **Wapiti**.
  * "Royal" removed in **1950** (Jan 26) after Independence.
  * **1979:** Crest text changed from "Indian Air Force" to **"भारतीय वायु सेना" (Bharatiya Vayu Sena)** in **Devanagari script**.
* **Facts:**
  * **Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon:** The **only IAF officer** to receive the **Param Vir Chakra (PVC)**.
  * **Highest Operational Fighter Airbase:** **Nomal Air Force Station**.
  * **Largest Airbase:** **Hindon Air Force Station**.
* **Aerobatic Teams:**
  * **Surya:** Hawk MK132.
  * **Sarang:** HAL Dhruv.
* **Special Commands:**
